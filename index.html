01 / The through-line

Different materials.
Same way of thinking.

The tools are changing. The care behind the work isn't.

My work spans structural steel, pipe systems, ladders, custom components, and heavy-duty truck repairs. Across shop and field roles, I've read blueprints, laid out and fitted parts, selected the right welding process, and handled large components safely.

I'm now looking to bring that same practical problem-solving and attention to detail into technology and coding. My next chapter builds on the habits that have guided every job so far: understand the requirements, work through the details, and deliver dependable work.

Good work is good work, whether it's measured in millimeters or written in lines of code.
